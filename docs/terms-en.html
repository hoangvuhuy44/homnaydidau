1. Scope of the service

Đi cà phê lets you browse a collection of Hanoi cafés, filter by area, choose a café yourself or pick one at random, and get a drink suggestion. The website does not accept reservations, orders or payments. You can use it without signing in.

2. Understanding the results

Random results are suggestions, not rankings, quality certifications or a guarantee that a café is open. The spinning effect and colours are for the experience only; there is no betting or cash-equivalent prize.

When a menu has been checked for the exact branch, drink suggestions come from checked items matching the filter, with a source and check date. Signature and house-recommendation labels require corresponding evidence. If no menu has been checked, the website clearly labels a drink as a general, unverified idea. Stale menus or menus without matching items are not filled with invented substitutes. The coffee photograph is illustrative, not a verified photograph of the selected café. Confirm the address, opening hours, prices and available drinks directly with the café before visiting.

You decide which drink suits you. If you have allergies or ingredient requirements, ask the café about ingredients and preparation before ordering.

Area filters use the 30 district and town areas from before 2025, rather than current administrative divisions. This collection does not include every café in Hanoi.

3. Google Maps and external links

The Maps button searches using the café name and saved address. Results may include a similarly named café or another branch. Confirm the location before travelling. External services have their own terms and privacy policies.

Café names, brands and links identify places. Inclusion does not imply a partnership, sponsorship or endorsement. Cafés and related services are responsible for their own products, transactions and support.
