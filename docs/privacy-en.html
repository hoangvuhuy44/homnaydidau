Privacy policy

What is stored when you choose a café and a drink.

Updated 11 September 2026

ScopeSelections and random resultsPreferences on your deviceTechnical data and providersOpening Google MapsYour choicesPolicy updates

1. Scope

This policy describes the current version of Đi cà phê. The website has no registration, sign-in, personal profile, information-collection form or payment feature.

2. Selections and random results

Café selection, drink selection, district filters and catalog searches run in your browser. The application does not send random results or Maps clicks to a separate analytics system or store a history of results on a server. The current result and filters exist only while the page is open and do not sync across devices.

The application does not request location permission or collect your GPS coordinates.

3. Preferences on your device

The website stores your light/dark choice in browser localStorage under coffee-theme, with the value light or dark. It also stores your language under coffee-language, with the value vi or en. These remember your preferences in the same browser on the same domain. The application does not send them to a server.

The application sets no automatic expiry for these preferences. They may remain until you clear website data or your browser removes them. You can clear them in your browser’s website-data settings. When reopened, the website uses the system theme and defaults to Vietnamese, and may store preferences again. Café selection still works if storage is blocked.

The application itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Infrastructure providers may use their own mechanisms to operate and protect their services.

4. Technical data and providers

When the website loads, hosting and content-delivery providers may process your IP address, browser information, request time and path to deliver content and protect the service. No sign-in does not mean no technical data is processed.

For a GitHub Pages version, GitHub states that it logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub’s privacy policy. For the chatgpt.site version, the hosting infrastructure and Cloudflare may process technical data under their policies.

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers and sends information required for that request, including your IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information. The illustrative photograph is served from the website’s own host.

Log retention and processing locations depend on each provider. This website does not set a common deletion deadline for data those providers manage.

5. Opening Google Maps

Your browser opens Maps only when you follow a Maps or collection link. Café search links include the café name and saved address, not coordinates collected by this application. Google may process visit data and permissions you have given its services under Google’s privacy policy.

Menu source links open only when clicked and are governed by the external website’s policy. Menu and correction links open your email application with the café name and address filled in; no email is sent until you send it yourself.

6. Your choices

You can change the theme or language, clear website data in your browser, or avoid opening external links. Clearing browser data removes local preferences but does not delete infrastructure providers’ logs. For data managed by an external service, use the support channel in that service’s policy.

7. Policy updates

This page will be updated when features or data handling change. The date below the title identifies the version. See also our Terms of use.
